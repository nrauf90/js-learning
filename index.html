About us

A till that keeps your books honest

Cashflow started from a simple idea: most small shops run the counter in one place and the accounts in another, so the two never quite agree at the end of the day.

So we built one app for both. Every sale you ring up moves stock and posts straight into your daily income — no double entry, no reconciling a drawer against a notebook.

Your data stays on your account, and the whole thing runs in a browser on whatever you already have at the counter — a phone, a tablet, or an old laptop.

How it works

Three steps to clarity

  1. 1

    Add your products

    Name, price and opening stock. Give each one a barcode or SKU if you scan at the counter.

  2. 2

    Sell at the till

    Search or scan onto the ticket, take cash or card, and Cashflow works out the change.

  3. 3

    Review your day

    Stock drops as you sell, sales post to your income, and the dashboard totals the week.
