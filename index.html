About us

Personal finance, made for everyday Pakistanis

Cashflow started from a simple idea: most of us juggle a salary, daily spending, and tax season — but the tools are either too complex or locked behind subscriptions.

We built a lightweight app that lets you log income and expenses by category, see your weekly balance on a dashboard, and estimate FBR salaried tax using published slab rates — without paying for the calculator.

Your data stays on your account. The tax tool runs entirely in your browser, so you can check your liability anytime, even without signing in.
