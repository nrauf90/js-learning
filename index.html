Stock aur munafa

Jo pata nahi, wo bhi batata hai

Most tills report an unrecorded cost as pure profit. That is how a shop ends up trusting a number that was never true. PK Galla reports it as unknown — separately, and out loud.

  • Har stock movement audited, running balance ke saath
  • Wastage typed by cause — kharab, expire, toota, chori
  • Recount wastage mein nahi ginta, kyunke maal tha hi nahi
  • Cost weighted average se chalti hai, har delivery par
  • Kaam ke waqt bhi — reports, khata aur till ek hi hisaab padhte hain

Bharosa

Hisaab wahi jo sach ho

No inflated claims here — just how the app is actually put together.

  • Har dukaan alag

    Every query is scoped to the shop that owns the data. Do dukaanon ka hisaab kabhi nahi milta.

  • Stock ka poora record

    Count sirf chaar raston se badalta hai, aur har baar ek row likhi jaati hai — kya hila, kyun, aur baad mein kitna bacha.

  • Golak milta hai

    Subah float, raat ko ginti. Expected aur actual ka farq wahin dikh jaata hai, baad mein nahi.

  • Staff ke apne login

    Munshi aur malik ke alag rights. Catalogue kaun badal sakta hai, ye aap tay karte hain.

  • Aap ka data, aap ka

    Reports PDF mein nikal lein jab chahein. Kuch bhi app ke andar phansa nahi rehta.

  • Jo pehle se hai, usi par

    Phone, tablet ya purana laptop — browser chahiye bas. Naya hardware kharidne ki zaroorat nahi.
