Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

PK Galla (“we”, “us”) operates a web application for daily expense tracking and a free Pakistan income tax calculator. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and password hash when you register.
  • Cash-flow data: income and expense entries, categories, dates, and optional notes you save.
  • Authentication: session tokens stored in your browser; optional Google OAuth profile data if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Tax calculator: runs in your browser. Income figures you enter for estimates are not sent to our servers unless you use share/URL features that encode values in the link.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and secure your account and cash-flow features.
  • Generate dashboards and reports for your entries.
  • Process subscriptions and payments when those features are enabled.
  • Respond to support requests you send to us.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with our payment provider and merchant of record (Paddle) only to complete transactions you initiate, or when required by law.

4. Storage & security

Account and entry data is stored on servers we control. We use industry-standard practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your choices

  • You may use the tax calculator without an account.
  • You may request account deletion by emailing support@cashflow.app.
  • You can clear local storage in your browser to remove saved auth tokens.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage for auth tokens, theme preference, and optional calculator settings. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

7. Children

PK Galla is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@cashflow.app
